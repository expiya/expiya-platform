Inspired by the Colors of Türkiye

Ayder

Ayder takes inspiration from the misty plateaus and deep green valleys of the Kaçkar highlands, where nature shows its most vivid shades.

Gemlik

Gemlik is where Togg is produced and its journey began. Included in the blue of Marmara, this geography represents the roots of the brand.

Anadolu

Anadolu color is inspired by the thousands of years of depth of these lands. Its warm and strong tone carries the traces of a rooted civilization.

Kula

Kula color is inspired by the volcanic geography of Manisa Kula. The millions of years of geological memory of Anatolia turns into a strong and characterful tone.

Kapadokya

Kapadokya color is inspired by the unique tones of fairy chimneys and natural rock formations. Nature's work of art comes to life on a modern surface.

Oltu

Oltu color is inspired by the unique Oltu stone of Erzurum. The noble stance of black coming from the deep history of Anatolia is reflected in this color.

Seyhan

Seyhan takes inspiration from the cotton fields across Çukurova's fertile plains. This distinctive white combines the pure beauty of our geography with functional simplicity.
