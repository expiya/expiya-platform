# Cars V3 decision/evidence boundary

`NEEDS_ONLY` is the default conversation mode. Money mentioned in this mode is conversation-scoped metadata and is excluded from filtering, ranking, readiness, question selection, offer ordering and the decision fingerprint. `BUDGET_AS_DECISION_FILTER` requires an explicit user transition and projects only a typed TRY ceiling into the decision input.

Equipment evidence has decision authority only for an explicit user requirement that is satisfied by an exact-variant, `VERIFIED`, `STANDARD` assertion. That narrow authority may eliminate candidates and may support a deterministic differentiating question. Association-only, optional/conditional, family capability, owner-manual capability and every other unverified record remain explanatory-only: they cannot eliminate or resurrect candidates, affect ranking or generate a decision question. Exact verified assertions may also produce a controlled badge. Association-only and family capability records produce an exact-version warning. Unknown or silent absence produces an unverifiable-information warning; it never proves absence. Conflicts and variants outside governed coverage produce no equipment claim. No internal assertion identifiers, checksums or governance payloads enter the public card.

The base deterministic decision projection excludes every equipment preference and excludes budget unless the explicit budget-filter mode is active. Equipment is evaluated afterward through the exact-verified-standard gate above and never enters ranking. Consequently unverified evidence coverage changes cannot change candidates, ranking, questions, readiness, offer order or revealed card identifiers.
